What is
Chinnara Chitra Chittara?

Chinnara Chitra Chittara is not just a drawing competition; it is a catalyst for self-belief. It intends to bring out the creativity and artistic expression from a child who feels sounds, instilling an "I can" attitude and breaking down inferiority complexes.

We believe that giving a pencil or a pen to an unschooled child who scribbles confidently reveals their raw artistic factor. Through scribbling, letters are formed, reading begins, and knowledge is built.

The main lookout of the Bannadamane family is not that every child must become an artist, but that this freedom of expression will help them prosper with a bright future in any aspect of life.

Our Vision

The Foundation of Every Great Mind Begins with a Sketch.

By giving a pencil to an unschooled child who scribbles confidently, we see their creativity. Through scribbling, letters are formed, reading begins, and knowledge grows.

An engineer first scribbles a building plan before it becomes a marvelous construction. A doctor must sketch anatomy to learn. Drawing is not just for artists; it is the universal language of innovation.

Important Guidelines

Absolute Freedom

Student is free to choose anything of their interest. Colouring can be done with crayons, water colours, pencil, pen, or charcoal.

School Routing Only

No individual entry. All drawings must be routed through schools. Organisers will visit your school to collect the drawings.

Originality is Key

The drawing has to be original and student-made. Teachers or parents can only guide them but must not involve in drawing.

International Selection Norms

Prizes will be given on the basis of 'International norms of selection' and 'Academy norms'. The judges' decision is final.
